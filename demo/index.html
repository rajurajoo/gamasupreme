One system, three businesses. Below is a walkthrough of how quotations, invoices, delivery, payroll and reporting run inside GAMA SUPREME — using live data from the working build, in AED, with UAE tax fields in place.

01 · Switching businesses

One login, three operating contexts

A single sign-in gives access to all three business lines. Switching context changes the document numbering, the nav, and which modules are relevant — but customers, employees and payroll stay shared across all three.

06 · Compliance

UAE e-invoicing — groundwork in place
